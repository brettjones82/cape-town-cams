Temperatures are adjusted for each summit's height. Wind on the peaks is the forecast at roughly summit height; the forecast has no gust figures up there, so those charts show the hourly average only.

Numbers come from Open-Meteo's weather and marine forecasts. Every chart uses the same scale, so bar heights compare across spots.

The forecast works in squares about 10 km across, so close-together spots can share one set of numbers. Each card says which spots share its square. Beach directions are approximate.
